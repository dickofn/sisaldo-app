Sisaldo · Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Applies to the Sisaldo Android app. Last updated 22 September 2026.

Sisaldo is a personal spending and credit-card tracker. It has no backend and no online database: everything you enter lives in a database file on your own device. The developer runs no server, receives no data from the app, and has no way to see what you record.

What the app stores, and where

Accounts, transactions, limits, budgets, rules and settings are written to a local SQLite file inside the app's private storage on the device. Nothing is sent anywhere by default. Uninstalling the app deletes that file.

Google Drive sync, only if you turn it on

You can connect your own Google account so the app keeps a copy of its data in Google Drive and can merge it with another device of yours. When you do, the app asks Google for exactly one permission:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

That scope covers only a hidden, app-specific folder in your Drive. The app cannot see, list or change any other file in your Drive, and no other app can see that folder. What it writes there is its own change log and periodic snapshots of the data described above, nothing else.

The sign-in happens between your device and Google directly. The resulting tokens are kept in the device's secure store and are never sent to the developer. You can disconnect at any time from the app's Settings, which deletes the tokens on the device, and you can remove the app's Drive access and its hidden folder from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Notifications and SMS, only if you grant them

On request, the app can read notifications and incoming SMS on the device in order to suggest a transaction when a bank or wallet reports a purchase. The matching runs entirely on the device. The message text is not stored, not synced and not sent anywhere; only the transaction you choose to save is kept, in the local database above. Both features are off until you grant them in Android's own settings, and you can revoke them there.

Network requests the app makes on its own

RequestToWhat it carries
Exchange-rate table A public CDN file (currency-api on jsDelivr, with a Cloudflare Pages mirror) Nothing about you. A fetch of a public, dated JSON file.
Update check GitHub, the public releases of dickofn/sisaldo-app Nothing about you. An anonymous request for the newest version number, and the APK if you choose to download it.
Drive sync Google Drive, your own account Your app data, to the hidden folder described above, and only after you connect.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.
  • No account with the developer. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No sale or sharing of data. The developer never holds any.

Deleting your data

On the device: uninstall the app, or use Settings inside the app to clear its data. In Google Drive: remove Sisaldo at the account permissions page linked above, which also deletes the hidden folder.

# Sisaldo

A spending and credit-card tracker for Android. Every figure stays on your own
phone: no account to make, no server, and no cloud unless you switch on Drive
sync yourself.

**[Download the newest APK](https://github.com/dickofn/sisaldo-app/releases/latest/download/sisaldo.apk)**
&nbsp;&middot;&nbsp;
**[Read the guide](https://dickofn.github.io/sisaldo-app/)**

The guide covers installing it, setting up your cards and limits, day-to-day
use, and how the numbers are worked out. Start there rather than here.

## What it does

- Tracks spending across any number of credit cards and cash accounts
- Works out what each card owes and how much room is left on it
- Handles several cards that share one credit limit between them
- Tracks progress toward a card's own perk, whether that is a usage count or a
  spend threshold
- Tells you what you have genuinely got left once everything you have committed
  to is accounted for
- Exports and imports your whole history as one file, and optionally syncs it to
  a hidden folder in your own Google Drive

## Before you install

The APK is signed with a debug key rather than a distribution key, so Android
will warn that it cannot recognise the publisher. That is deliberate: the key's
fingerprint is what the Google sign-in client used for Drive sync is registered
against. The [guide](https://dickofn.github.io/sisaldo-app/) explains it in full.
If that trade is not one you want to accept, do not install it.
